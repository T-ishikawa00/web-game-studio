Privacy

Tinlumo Studio is individually operated. This page explains how information is used to provide games and advertising and respond to inquiries.

There are no accounts, purchases or cloud saves. We do not ask you to enter your age or date of birth. Tinlumo’s game functions and optional analytics preference do not create persistent identifiers to track visitors or use device fingerprinting. See Advertising for how advertising services handle information.

Storage on your device

Your browser’s localStorage keeps game progress, completions and preferences such as reduced motion, mute, volume, Hakorilo music settings and chosen language. The saved items vary by game. These let you continue playing and keep your preferences. They have no automatic expiry. Preferences saved by earlier versions, such as dismissed guides, may also remain.

Saves are specific to the same device, browser and site. They do not transfer to another device or browser. Clearing site data, private browsing or storage restrictions may cause you to lose progress and preferences.

Resetting progress in Hakorilo, Lightwork Garden or Two Ways Home removes that game’s progress, completions and reduced-motion setting. Use the site Settings page for Hakorilo, or the in-game settings for the other two games. Sound, guide and language preferences remain. To remove all stored data, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

Temporary in-game interaction information is held in device memory and is discarded when the page is unloaded, apart from the progress and preferences each game saves. This interaction information is not sent externally.

Island builder saves

Buildings, resources, progress, placements and language, sound, motion and graphics settings save automatically in this browser. Resetting the island keeps your settings. A message appears if saving is unavailable. Progress is shared across Japanese and English; your saved language preference takes priority.

PROJECT MAGNET saves

Mission unlocks, records, language, mute, motion preferences and a sector-start checkpoint are saved in this browser. Resetting PROJECT MAGNET deletes its settings as well as its progress. Other games are unaffected. If saving is unavailable, the game tells you and lets you continue in this tab only.

RIMBLOOM records

RIMBLOOM stores your best score, best chain, previous score and run count in this browser’s localStorage. Runs in progress are not saved or resumed. Erase these records in the RIMBLOOM section of site Settings. Other games’ saves and your analytics choice remain unchanged. If storage is unavailable, records last only for this page.

Optional analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is off when you first use a browser. We do not automatically show an analytics prompt. It turns on only if you open Analytics settings in the footer and choose Allow analytics. All game features remain available if you make no choice or decline analytics.

An analytics choice previously saved in this browser is kept. We store your allow or decline preference in localStorage without creating a visitor ID. It has no automatic expiry and can be changed in Analytics settings. Opening settings or closing them without choosing does not give consent. Clearing site data may remove game progress and preferences as well as your analytics choice.

If you allow analytics, page URLs, referrers, browser and device information, and performance information such as loading speed are sent to Cloudflare to help improve page use and performance. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or localStorage to collect usage metrics or fingerprint individuals. Tinlumo's localStorage preference is separate from Cloudflare's measurement method. In-game actions and game-save data are not sent to analytics.

Withdrawing permission reloads the page and stops further measurement. Requests already started may finish. Blocked analytics or connection failures do not restrict play. Cloudflare handles and retains information under its Privacy Policy and related terms. Tinlumo does not guarantee a fixed retention period or immediate deletion. Connection information needed to deliver the site is still processed when optional analytics is off.

This choice covers only Cloudflare Web Analytics and is independent of advertising consent managed through Google's CMP. Your analytics choice does not give or decline advertising consent. See Advertising on this page for information about ads.

Advertising

Tinlumo uses Google AdSense, and ads may be displayed.

Google and third-party advertising vendors and ad networks involved through AdSense may set and read cookies and use web beacons, IP addresses and similar technologies to collect and use information for purposes such as delivering and measuring ads.

Tinlumo uses Google's setting to automatically include commonly used ad partners. The partners change as Google updates the set. For the latest partner lists and information about individual providers, see Google's ad technology partners (ATPs) list. When Google's consent message is displayed in the relevant regions, you can review the partners selected for Tinlumo on its Ad partners page.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may serve ads based on your previous visits to Tinlumo or other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable Google and its partners to display ads based on these visits. Personalized ads may be displayed; the actual processing depends on your region, consent choices, advertising settings and other factors.

You can turn off Google's personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. This does not hide all ads and is separate from withdrawing advertising consent on Tinlumo.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, we use Google's European regulations message (a consent management platform, or CMP) to obtain and manage the necessary consent for advertising. To change or withdraw your consent choices, use the Google consent message when displayed, or the advertising privacy settings link at the bottom of the page if it is available.

Google's CMP and Analytics settings (Consent C) for Cloudflare Web Analytics are separate systems. Allowing or declining analytics does not change your Google advertising consent, and advertising consent does not change your analytics choice.

Information you send us

We use the email address, message, attachments and other information you send, together with our reply history, to review your inquiry, respond when needed and handle requests under applicable law. Tinlumo does not use this information for advertising or AI training.

We use Cloudflare Email Routing to receive email and Google’s Gmail to store messages and reply. Replies may come from a different email address.

Inquiry messages are normally deleted around 90 days after resolution. We review unresolved inquiries monthly. We may retain information longer when reasonably needed for legal obligations, security incidents, abuse or disputes. Copies may remain in the email provider’s trash or backups, so immediate complete deletion is not guaranteed.

Deleted Gmail messages move to Trash and are normally permanently deleted after 30 days. Google’s general deletion process usually takes around two months, and encrypted backups may retain data for up to six months. Legal exceptions and delays may apply, so a deletion completion date cannot be guaranteed for an individual message. Google uses servers worldwide and may process information outside your country.

Optional game measurement

Published: 2026-10-03

Optional game measurement is sent only after explicit permission in the separate consent control. Collection, retention and withdrawal follow the terms shown above. Events are received at https://tinlumo.com/__growth/events and stored in Cloudflare D1. The app does not store IP addresses, User-Agent, referrer or free-form URL data as event records, and provides no public reading API. Cloudflare handles network information required to process traffic. Workers Logs and Traces are disabled in this account. Existing Cloudflare Free Security Analytics retain adaptive samples for 7 days, and Security Events for 24 hours, including request time, IP, host and path. These are provider traffic-protection records, separate from the optional game-event records in D1. Event-body logging and Worker Observability are disabled. Reading and aggregation are restricted to authorized operators of the Owner's Cloudflare account. CSV exports are not publicly served.

Optional game measurement. For Hakorilo and RIMBLOOM, Tinlumo receives a page-present observation after consent, readiness transitions after consent, first interaction, trial start, result, retry and startup failure events, plus the game, release, language, input type and selected campaign. We do not send boards, coordinate trails, recordings, free text or a persistent user ID. A document ID is created and held in browser memory and stops being used after at most 30 minutes. It is not stored in cookies or localStorage. Sent document IDs and event IDs are stored by the receiver with their events for the retention periods below. Each tab is a separate document. Activity before consent is neither buffered nor replayed, including readiness that completed before consent.

Received events are used for aggregation for up to 7 days. An hourly deletion job normally removes expired rows from the active table; expired rows may normally remain in the active table for up to one hour until the next hourly job, but are excluded from aggregation immediately. New collection stops if a deletion job fails or leaves a backlog, a row remains at least one hour beyond expiry, or the last successful health check is over two hours old. After an outage, deletion resumes in batches of at most 2,000 rows per invocation, and reception stays stopped until the backlog clears. Cloudflare D1 Free recovery copies may retain deleted rows for up to another 7 days. Withdrawal stops future sending and discards unsent records. Records already received follow these retention periods. This choice is separate from Cloudflare page analytics Consent C and advertising consent. All games remain playable if you decline.
