## Extraction

Use short encounter chapters (Brotato/Grindstone), trajectory/positioning climax (Roundguard/Beast Breaker), and mode-specific mastery records (Geometry Wars/Splasher). Add seeded layouts and a harder route without permanent stat inflation. The sources support structural choices; the 5–8-minute RIMBLOOM design target and fun must be established by its own play evidence. No claim that all 24 games were played or that marketing copy proves their quality.
