# Complete Edition product decision

Fixed pillars: FAST, RESPONSIVE, CHAINABLE, READABLE, IMMEDIATE RETRY. Add progression, escalation, finale, mastery and replayability. Preserve launch speed 1450 px/s, dash .62 s, recovery .23 s, wall charge, armor block, relay propagation, landing preview, audio/VFX language and JA/EN.

## Options evaluated

|Dimension|A: Continuous Garden Journey — PRIMARY|B: Objective ladder|C: Survival with draft upgrades|
|---|---|---|---|
|Flow|Six escalating gardens, then three crown circuits and Result|Nine short rooms, clear quota then next room|Six timed waves, three upgrade choices, boss|
|Stages/progression|50/55/60/60/65/70 s; advance after 86% plus quota or full timer; finale up to 90 s|30–70 s per room; player performance drives progress|Six 60 s waves plus choices and finale|
|Chains|Small fans → armor/relay lanes → moving crescents → dense setup|Fixed puzzles reward chosen angle|Upgrade combinations increase pulse/shot density|
|Enemies/obstacles|Skaters, telegraphed landing thorns, inner reflecting rails, crown seals|Curated blockers and quotas|Buffs change speed/range/pulse rules|
|Failure|Three life; one life restored each garden; finale deadline|Life or missed objective|Life or boss deadline|
|Clear/finale|12 marked seals across 3 circuits; wall/rail charge required, all HP=1|Final designed room of goals|Boss weak points|
|Replay|Route-specific records and rank goals, seeds, Wild route, Quick Run|Room medals, time attack|Draft builds and random effects|
|Expected session|5–8 min design/copy; first expert actual clear 5:21; current timer maximum 7:30|5–10 min estimate, high skill may fall below 5|7–12 min including decisions estimate|
|Production cost|Moderate; shared core, seven authored schedules|High; each quota/room needs robust solution/fairness proof|High; every combination needs balance and UI|
|Mobile|Same drag/release and preview, no mid-run menus|Strong, but small objectives can burden screen|Choice panels and altered prediction add burden|
|Dilution risk|Low: unchanged movement and uninterrupted stages|Medium: quotas may replace free chaining with chores|High: speed/range mutations disturb accepted cadence|

Choose A. It teaches through encounter arrangement and preserves free play. The player's ability grows; core physics do not. Stage changes wait for the dash to settle, clear old threats, grant brief safety and restore one life, without a cutscene or click. No account, inventory, shop, grind, backend, BGM or permanent power tree.

Mastery advances each of the first six gardens once both 86% of its authored timer and its kill quota are met. Quotas are 32/40/44/44/60/70. Otherwise the full timer advances the garden, so a missed quota never locks the player out. The minimum six-garden time is 309.6 seconds; the full authored total is 360 seconds. Dash-settle rounding remains bounded by the global 450-second deadline. Crown has its own 90-second deadline, also capped by the global deadline; its clear is immediate when all 12 seals break. Players keep attacking during stage changes and circuit reset.

## Authored progression

1. First Bloom (50 s): clean soft fans and relay introduction, roomy landing.
2. Gilded Rim (55 s): armor gates beside relays; wall first, then chain.
3. Drifting Petals (60 s): visibly marked skaters cross the aim lane; predict travel.
4. Thornlight (60 s): outlined thorns warn before becoming active; dash safely through, land outside.
5. Mirror Garden (65 s): two thin luminous internal rails reflect and charge; prediction matches collision.
6. High Tide (70 s): rails plus moving targets, guarded relay formations and landing thorns; bounded density and periodic recovery windows.
7. Crown Bloom (up to 90 s): three circuits of four marked armor seals, framed by relays. Each seal breaks in one charged contact. Circuit layout changes, a landing hazard is telegraphed, then a new route opens. Break all 12 for clear. Surviving the timer alone does not clear. The final crown bloom and finite clear chord give closure; Result shows route, stage, chain, armor, safe landings, score rank and immediate retry.

Normal restores one life per stage, capped at three. Wild raises pursuit/pressure and removes stage healing; available immediately, separate record. Assist slows pressure/pursuit without changing projectile feel; separate record. Normal is default. No run starts automatically from a remembered advanced route.

Saving: validated namespaced per-route journey records, Quick Run's accepted record key retained. Settings saved locally. No personal data/network. In-progress runs are intentionally not persisted; refresh returns to menu. Score ranks are mastery goals, not unlock requirements. A = clear + 65,000 points + chain 8; S = clear + 100,000 points + chain 12; B = clear + 35,000 points; C = other clears. Rank never prevents a clear.

Actual evidence: the first skilled trusted-mouse clear reached 7/7 and 12/12 seals in 321.46 active seconds, score 74,780, chain 9, rank A, life 3. Crown lasted 10.55 seconds for this agent's geometrically guided play. The original canvas/SE recording includes substantial pauses between input batches, with a 17:15 decoded recording duration; it is not a continuous five-minute video. The 5–8-minute description is a design target, not a measured population average. Typical novice completion time, retention and real-device performance remain NOT VERIFIED.
