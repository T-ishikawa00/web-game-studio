# Audio, accessibility and JA/EN

Original Web Audio tones mark charge tiers, launch, landing, checkpoint, fall, clear and summit. Audio unlocks through user interaction. No autoplay soundtrack, narration, microphone or external audio. Sound is switchable/persisted; voices/notes are bounded. Capture uses the game's audio bus. Final MP4s have AAC-LC stereo 48kHz audio with peaks -21.6/-21.6/-21.3 dBFS; listening/device compatibility is not verified.

Hold/release the pointer anywhere on the field, or Space while focused, to charge/hop. No air steering. Escape pauses/resumes or closes the map. Pointer cancellation/lost capture, visibility loss and cross-tab reset safely cancel held charge; stale release does not launch. Descending foot overlap and declared next-route geometry determine landing.

Utilities, course buttons and reset targets are at least 44px. Canvas/frame have visible keyboard focus. Course-map enabled buttons wrap focus. Instructions, unlock/progress, failure/retry, storage notices and charge/live messages are Japanese/English. ?lang=ja|en supplies entry language; switching persists a preference and announces the new language, clearing old-language toast.

Reduced effects follows the system initially and can be overridden. It removes decoration/particles/shake/easing; essential pad/camera motion remains. Visible hop/landing/retry states do not depend on audio. Ordinary browser Space/Escape/pointer/pause/settings/failure/retry/save/reset were checked, plus source cancellation/storage tests. This is not screen-reader/assistive-device certification. Physical touch, iOS/Safari and device audio remain NOT TESTED.
