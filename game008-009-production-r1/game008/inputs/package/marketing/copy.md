# COILHOP marketing copy

## Japanese

**Title:** COILHOP

**Description:** バネを縮め、離してジャンプ。動く踏み台と二つの分かれ道を越え、八つの灯りの庭を登って山頂の鐘を鳴らそう。

**Controls:** Spaceキーまたは画面を押し続け、離すとジャンプ。空中では進路を変えられません。

**Hooks:**

- 押す長さで変わるジャンプ。短い弧と遠い弧を踏み台に合わせて使い分ける。
- 灯台に着地すると、落下してもそこから再挑戦できる。
- 二つのコースに、上と下から選ぶ分かれ道がある。
- 動く足場を見極め、最後は山頂の鐘へ。

## English

**Title:** COILHOP

**Description:** Hold to compress the spring, then release to hop. Read moving ledges, take either of two real route forks, and climb eight lantern gardens to ring the summit bell.

**Controls:** Hold Space or press and hold the play field; release to jump. You cannot steer in the air.

**Hooks:**

- Each hold changes the hop. Tune short and long arcs to the next ledge.
- Lit beacons let you retry nearby after a fall.
- Two courses give you a choice between higher and lower routes.
- Read moving rests and ring the bell at the summit.

## Artwork note

The wide and square covers are original vector illustrations of COILHOP's copper spring courier, garden ledges, lanterns, and summit bell. They are illustrative promotional artwork, not gameplay screenshots or recorded-play evidence. The only visible promotional copy in the images is “COILHOP” and “Hold. Release. Land.” They contain no scores, awards, player counts, or other product claims.
