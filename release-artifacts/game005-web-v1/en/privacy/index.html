Privacy

Tinlumo Studio is individually operated. This page explains how information is used to provide the games and respond to inquiries.

There are no accounts, purchases, ads or cloud saves. We do not ask you to enter your age or date of birth. We do not create persistent identifiers to track visitors or use device fingerprinting.

Storage on your device

Your browser’s localStorage keeps each game’s progress and completions, reduced-motion setting, mute and volume, Hakorilo music settings, dismissed guides and chosen language. These let you continue playing and keep your preferences. They have no automatic expiry.

Saves are specific to the same device, browser and site. They do not transfer to another device or browser. Clearing site data, private browsing or storage restrictions may cause you to lose progress and preferences.

For Hakorilo, Lightwork Garden and Two Ways Home, “Erase this game’s progress” removes that game’s progress, completions and reduced-motion setting. Sound, guide and language preferences remain. To remove all stored data, clear this site’s data in your browser settings.

sessionStorage holds temporary display and direction-button preferences and information that avoids recording the same session start twice. This may remain when a browser restores a tab. In-game interaction information is held temporarily in device memory, discarded when the page is unloaded and not sent externally.

Island builder saves

Buildings, resources, progress, placements and language, sound, motion and graphics settings save automatically in this browser. Resetting the island keeps your settings. A message appears if saving is unavailable. Progress is shared across Japanese and English; your saved language preference takes priority.

PROJECT MAGNET saves

Mission unlocks, records, language, mute, motion preferences and a sector-start checkpoint are saved in this browser. Resetting PROJECT MAGNET deletes its settings as well as its progress. Other games are unaffected. If saving is unavailable, the game tells you and lets you continue in this tab only.

Hosting

Cloudflare delivers and protects the site. For these purposes, it may process IP addresses and connection information associated with page requests.

Cloudflare may process and transfer information worldwide. Its privacy policy identifies the United States and European Economic Area (EEA) as its primary storage locations. Retention depends on the purpose, necessity and legal obligations. Tinlumo does not separately store access logs, but Cloudflare may still process and retain this information.

Optional analytics

Cloudflare Web Analytics is off when you first use a browser. We do not automatically show an analytics prompt. It turns on only if you open Analytics settings in the footer and choose Allow analytics. All game features remain available if you make no choice or decline analytics.

An analytics choice previously saved in this browser is kept. We store your allow or decline preference in localStorage without creating a visitor ID. It has no automatic expiry and can be changed in Analytics settings. Opening settings or closing them without choosing does not give consent. Clearing site data may remove game progress and preferences as well as your analytics choice.

If you allow analytics, page URLs, referrers, browser and device information, and performance information such as loading speed are sent to Cloudflare to help improve page use and performance. Cloudflare states that it does not use cookies or localStorage to collect usage metrics or fingerprint individuals. Tinlumo's localStorage preference is separate from Cloudflare's measurement method. In-game actions and game-save data are not sent to analytics.

Withdrawing permission reloads the page and stops further measurement. Requests already started may finish. Blocked analytics or connection failures do not restrict play. Cloudflare handles and retains information under its Privacy Policy and related terms. Tinlumo does not guarantee a fixed retention period or immediate deletion. Connection information needed to deliver the site is still processed when optional analytics is off.

This choice covers only Cloudflare Web Analytics and is independent of advertising consent. We do not currently serve ads. Before introducing ads, we will provide information and choices appropriate to the relevant regions and processing.

Information you send us

We use the email address, message, attachments and other information you send, together with our reply history, to review your inquiry, respond when needed and handle requests under applicable law. Tinlumo does not use this information for advertising or AI training.

We use Cloudflare Email Routing to receive email and Google’s Gmail to store messages and reply. Replies may come from a different email address.

Inquiry messages are normally deleted around 90 days after resolution. We review unresolved inquiries monthly. We may retain information longer when reasonably needed for legal obligations, security incidents, abuse or disputes. Copies may remain in the email provider’s trash or backups, so immediate complete deletion is not guaranteed.

Deleted Gmail messages move to Trash and are normally permanently deleted after 30 days. Google’s general deletion process usually takes around two months, and encrypted backups may retain data for up to six months. Legal exceptions and delays may apply, so a deletion completion date cannot be guaranteed for an individual message. Google uses servers worldwide and may process information outside your country.
